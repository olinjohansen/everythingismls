TeX Gyre Heros
==============

The fonts in this directory (heros-regular.woff2, heros-bold.woff2) are
derived from TeX Gyre Heros, a free, metric-compatible clone of Helvetica
based on URW Nimbus Sans L.

Copyright 2007–2009 by B. Jackowski and J.M. Nowacki (on behalf of TeX
Users Groups). Distributed under the GUST Font License (GFL), a free and
open license. See: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/licenses

These woff2 files were generated from the official OTF distribution at:
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros

They are used here as the on-screen face for headlines, providing true
Helvetica letterforms consistently across all platforms. Licensed
"Helvetica Neue" / Helvetica / Arial remain CSS fallbacks.
